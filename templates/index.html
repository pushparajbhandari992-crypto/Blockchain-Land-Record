HOW IT WORKS

From Registration to Verification

LandChain uses a simple digital workflow backed by blockchain-style hashing.

01

Register

Enter the required land and ownership information into the system.

02

Hash

Important record information can be converted into a SHA-256 digital hash.

03

Store

The land record and transaction information are stored in the system.

04

Verify

The system can verify the record and check its blockchain history.

ABOUT LANDCHAIN

A Modern Approach to Digital Land Records

LandChain is an academic prototype that demonstrates how blockchain concepts can be applied to digital land record management.

Each important transaction can be connected to the previous transaction using cryptographic hashes, creating a transparent chain of records.
